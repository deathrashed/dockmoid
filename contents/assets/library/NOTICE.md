# Bundled service icons

The 113 service icons that match filenames in the [Dashboard Icons project](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons) are sourced from that project under the Apache License 2.0. The same project supplies the optional downloadable library in the Services settings page; its license is included alongside the downloaded files.

Six bundled logos have no Dashboard Icons match and remain from the [selfh.st icon collection](https://github.com/selfhst/icons) under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): `beets.svg`, `cross-seed.svg`, `homepage.svg`, `kometa.svg`, `soulseek.svg`, and `speedtest-tracker.svg`.

The logos are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify the software a container runs.
