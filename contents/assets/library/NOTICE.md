# Bundled service icons

These icons come from the [selfh.st icon collection](https://github.com/selfhst/icons),
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They are a small subset of the most common self-hosted services, included so the
widget shows real logos out of the box. The full collection (about 3,000 icons)
can be downloaded from the widget's Icons settings page.

The logos themselves are trademarks of their respective owners and are used
here only to identify the software a container runs.
